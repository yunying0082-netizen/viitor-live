# ViiTor Live — Free Audio & Video Transcription

**Turn every recording into clear, usable text.**

[ViiTor Live](https://www.viitor.live/zh-CN) is a free, browser-based transcription tool that converts audio and video into editable, searchable text. It helps students, researchers, content creators, and teams turn recordings into useful content without repeatedly searching through timelines.

**Try it now: https://www.viitor.live/zh-CN**

## ✨ Features

- **Audio & Video Transcription** — Transcribe MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4, MOV, and other supported formats. Maximum file size: 500 MB per file.
- **Public Media Import** — Import supported public media links from YouTube, Bilibili, TikTok, Douyin, and other sources, subject to platform restrictions.
- **Speaker Identification** — Automatically label speakers and edit their names.
- **Timestamped Transcripts** — Jump to specific moments in the original recording to review and verify text.
- **Custom Vocabulary** — Add names, brands, and technical terms to improve recognition of specialized vocabulary.
- **AI Summaries** — Extract key points from long recordings.
- **Export Options** — Export transcripts as DOCX, TXT, or SRT subtitles.
- **Text-Based Audio Editing** — Remove unwanted spoken sentences, edit text, generate replacement speech, and preview the result.

## 🌍 Supported Languages

ViiTor Live supports over 100 languages, including English, Japanese, Spanish, Mandarin, Cantonese, and Wu Chinese.

Transcription accuracy may vary depending on audio quality, accents, background noise, and overlapping speech.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open [ViiTor Live](https://www.viitor.live/zh-CN).
2. Upload an audio or video file, or import a supported public media link.
3. Select the language and configure speaker identification if needed.
4. Add custom vocabulary when necessary.
5. Start transcription and review the timestamped text.
6. Generate an AI summary or export your transcript.

## 👥 Who Is It For?

- Students and educators working with lectures and study materials.
- Researchers conducting interviews and analyzing recordings.
- Podcasters and content creators preparing transcripts and subtitles.
- Teams organizing meetings, discussions, and voice recordings.

## ⚠️ Limitations

ViiTor Live processes recordings online, so files must be uploaded to its servers. Recognition may contain errors, and public link imports depend on platform availability. Important information should be verified against the original recording. Text-based audio editing focuses on spoken content rather than professional multitrack production.

Consider your data security requirements before uploading confidential recordings.
